Welcome To Evergreen

A Peaceful Haven in Dahanu's Chikoo Country

Escape the bustling city life of Mumbai and Gujarat to Hotel Evergreen Restaurant & Rooms — where nature, comfort, and coastal flavors harmonize.

Dahanu is world-renowned for its sweet GI-tagged chikoos and pristine Arabian Sea beaches. Hotel Evergreen is strategically set amidst verdant chikoo groves and coconut palms, offering an authentic retreat for families, couples, corporate teams, and destination wedding hosts.

Whether you are taking a dip in our sparkling swimming pool, enjoying mouth-watering fresh Surmai fry or Agri chicken in our garden restaurant, camping under starlit skies with a warm bonfire, or picking organic Chikoos from our orchards, we ensure every moment is memorable.

    Getting Here

    Easily Accessible in Dahanu

    Located in the tranquil Asave / Dahanu region, just minutes from the railway station and connected via smooth highways to Mumbai and Gujarat.

    Travel Directions & Distances

    From Dahanu Road Railway Station

    Just 8-12 minutes drive (approx. 5 km). Direct Western Railway local trains from Churchgate/Borivali and express trains from Mumbai & Gujarat.

    From Mumbai / Thane (NH-48)

    Around 130 km via Mumbai-Ahmedabad National Highway 48. A scenic 2.5 to 3 hours drive with smooth toll roads.

    From Vapi / Surat

    Approx. 55 km from Vapi (1 hour) and 160 km from Surat. Perfect weekend escape for Gujarat travelers.

    Nearby Attractions

    Dahanu Beach (15 mins), Bordi Beach (25 mins), Asavali Dam, Bahrot Caves, and ancient Parsi Fire Temple in Udvada.
